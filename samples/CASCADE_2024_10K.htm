Cascade Foods Corporation

For the fiscal year ended September 28, 2024. Fictional company for software testing.

Item 1. Business

Cascade Foods Corporation produces frozen meals and plant-based snacks sold through grocery chains and club stores. Our two largest retail customers together represented 31% of total revenues in fiscal 2024.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Commodity costs for wheat, soy and packaging can rise quickly. A 10% increase in commodity costs would have reduced fiscal 2024 gross profit by about $54 million.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenues rose to $2,481.2 million in fiscal 2024. We opened a new plant in Boise, Idaho in fiscal 2024 and plan capital expenditures of $150 million to $160 million in fiscal 2025. Our revolving credit facility of $300 million was undrawn at year end.
